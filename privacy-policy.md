# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 September 2026_

## 1. Scope

Gustavo ("the Tool") is an internal tool used only by its owners. It has no
public users, no sign-up, and collects no personal data from anyone other than
its own operators.

## 2. What is stored

The Tool runs locally on its operators' own computers. It stores, on that
machine only:

- **Access and refresh tokens** for the social media accounts its operators
  have connected, so it does not have to ask for authorization on every use.
- **Account identifiers and display names** of those same accounts.
- **Public performance metrics** of the operators' own posts (views, likes,
  comments, shares), to measure their own content.

Nothing is stored about any other person.

## 3. What is not done

- No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
- No advertising, profiling, or tracking of any kind.
- No data about other platform users is collected, scraped, or retained.
- No data leaves the operators' own machine, except API calls to the platforms
  themselves.

## 4. Retention and deletion

Tokens and metrics are kept only as long as the Tool is in use. Deleting the
Tool's local database removes them. Authorization can also be revoked at any
time from the connected account's own settings, which immediately invalidates
the stored tokens.

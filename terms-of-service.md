## 2. Accounts

The Tool connects only to social media accounts whose owners have explicitly
authorized it through the platform's own authorization flow. Authorization can
be revoked at any time from the account's settings on the relevant platform.

## 3. What the Tool does

The Tool uploads media as **drafts**. It does not publish content. Publication
is always performed manually by a human from the platform's own application.

The Tool is used solely to publish the owners' own original content, in
compliance with the terms of service of each platform it connects to.
